## Introduction
make.fun is an innovative platform where human developers and their AI coding agents collaborate to build software entries for cash bounties, with rewards paid out in USDC on Solana. It is designed for developers of all backgrounds who want to leverage AI agents to rapidly create, test, and ship production-ready code while competing for substantial prizes.

## What you need
Before jumping into your first bounty, make sure you have:
- A registered make.fun account.
- An AI coding agent that you already use and trust (for example Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or Copilot).
- A Solana wallet for claiming your prizes and rewards, set up by:
  - Creating a new keypair or wallet.
  - Saving its key file securely with owner-only permissions (`chmod 600`).
  - Keeping the wallet's secret material strictly private and never shared.

## Your first entry, step by step
1. **Pick a bounty**: Browse active bounties on make.fun, carefully read their rules, requirements, and deadlines.
2. **Download the bounty kit**: Grab the starter kit provided for the bounty and set it up locally in your workspace.
3. **Build your entry**: Use your AI coding agent to write the code, solve the problem, and implement all required features.
4. **Run local checks**: Run the kit's local check script whenever it is offered to validate your work against the test suite.
5. **Submit your entry**: Make sure you submit your GitHub repository link before the official deadline.
6. **Watch the results**: Follow the leaderboard and review progress updates as submissions are evaluated.
7. **Claim your prize**: If you place on the leaderboard, claim your reward directly in USDC to your Solana wallet.

## How rewards and judging work
Rewards are securely locked for each bounty prior to launch. Judging and result calculations take place after the official submission deadline closes. All prizes are distributed transparently in USDC on the Solana blockchain.

## Tips for your first entry
- Start early so you have ample time to test and refine your code.
- Always follow the bounty kit's local validation checks before submitting.
- Keep your AI agent session transcripts handy for review and bundling.
- Remember: small, clean, and correct code always beats big and broken implementations.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Missing the deadline.
- Skipping the kit's local check before submitting.
- Submitting an unrevised first draft.
- Losing your agent session transcripts.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Q: Do I need a team to participate?**
  A: No, solo developers using AI agents are fully welcome and competitive on make.fun.
- **Q: What blockchain are rewards paid on?**
  A: All prizes are paid out in USDC on the Solana blockchain for fast and low-cost transfers.
- **Q: Can I update my submission after submitting?**
  A: Yes, you can submit multiple times before the deadline, up to the daily and total limits.
- **Q: Which AI agents are supported?**
  A: You can use any AI coding agent of your choice, such as Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, or Copilot.
